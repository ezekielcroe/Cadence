# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 14, 2026

## 1. Overview

**Cadence** ("the App") is designed with a philosophy of **Data Sovereignty**. We believe that your time and your journal entries are personal assets. Therefore, the App is architected to be "Local-First." We do not operate a backend server, we do not create user accounts, and we do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.

## 2. Data Collection and Storage

### 2.1 Local Storage

All data entered into Cadence—including Activity definitions, Time Blocks, Remarks, and Day Notes—is stored locally on your device using Apple's **SwiftData** framework.

* **Ownership:** You own your data. You can export it at any time via the "Data Management" settings in CSV or JSON format.
* **Access:** We (the developers) have no access to your database.

### 2.2 iCloud Backups

If you have **iCloud Backup** enabled on your device, your local app data is included in those backups. This data is encrypted by Apple and is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not have access to these backups.

### 2.3 User Defaults

The App uses the device's local `UserDefaults` storage to save simple preferences, such as:

* Interface theme settings (Light/Dark mode).
* Notification preferences.
* Tracking timer state (to recover sessions after the app is backgrounded).

## 3. App Permissions

### 3.1 Notifications

Cadence requests permission to send **Local Notifications**.

* **Purpose:** To alert you when an activity has been auto-stopped, or to remind you of your Morning Intention/Evening Reflection.
* **Privacy:** These notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to a push notification server.

## 4. Analytics and Third-Party Services

**Cadence contains no third-party tracking code.**

* No Google Analytics / Firebase.
* No Facebook / Meta Pixel.
* No advertising SDKs.
* No crash reporting services that automatically upload logs.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the App does not collect your email address, we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Continued use of the App implies acceptance of the updated policy.
